### 7.20.2023

## AdminiTruth's Origin Story

Karen stared into her full length mirror and rotated through the new outfits she had bought for the mission. They were all plays on the "women in black" theme her, Raquel, and Adria had come up with in preparation for the months to come. Adria, the actual former CIA officer, thought this was silly of Karen and Raquel, but of course Adria did, she was the real deal - Karen was just having fun, and besides, very few people would see Karen in costume as her assignment had her working from home, ready to watch The Man's every move in the childhood home he would soon be returning to at the start of next month. For good measure, Will Smith's _Men In Black_ blared in the background on repeat. Karen loved a good, multi tiered reference.

Karen, now in her mid fifties, had at some point held producer and executive producer level positions at nearly every major news network, even the conservative ones, along with similar positions at many cable channels that handled live programming of all types of varieties. As a result, Karen knew someone or at the least knew someone who knew someone in every control room throughout the country, and many outside of it as well for good measure. This was, of course, why she was selected for the cause, and she took pause to remember it, recalling the day it all began.

...

It was early 2018 - Art Auerbach, after securing Karen's contract as per his role as her new talent agent, invited Karen to play golf with none other than the previous President of the United States, and Art's husband, Roman. Karen had never been happier to have taken those golf lessons a few years ago, and have kept it up every summer or so. As a result, she had to hold back, particularly around the former POTUS, who lacked a similar talent for the sport. It didn't matter as he was doing most of the talking.

"Karen," the former POTUS began, "I'm so glad you could join us today. While I hope you're enjoying the outing, as am I, it's not really the point of our meeting today. I'd like to discuss that, if that's alright with you."

"Of course, Mr. President!" stammered Karen, praying she wasn't blushing.

"Great - Karen, as I'm sure you're aware, this country is under siege. The combat taking place isn't physical, or you'd be helping Raquel report on it over at the Pequod - the combat is intellectual. Karen, the very nature of the truth is at stake here - and the stakes are high."

Karen tensed up, her sets of teeth biting down on each other as she did so. She was no longer afraid of blushing - this was it, and it seemed to be a big deal. The former POTUS continued.

"I'm not sure how else to put this - we're forming an organization, which in one level is overt, but it's true nature is and will always be covert - kept a secret. We're recruiting you to play a role on both sides - just like myself, Art, and Roman."

Karen looked around - everyone had stopped focusing on their clubs. In the background, part of the former POTUS' Secret Service detail delayed the next group of golfers, whom seemed more than happy to oblige.

"`The Other Party` has an obvious media presence, with a network of channels that are clearly affiliated to their ideological image - as do we at `the Inner Party`. It's no secret that we lost the last election, and we're seeking to regain an advantage here. We believe `The Other Party` has beaten us almost explicitly through lying. You agree, of course, don't you Karen?"

Karen agreed immediately, allowing the former POTUS to continue yet again.

"What we at `the Inner Party` have decided to do is to go underground, so to speak, to help bring power back to the truth. What we want to do is install a network of like minds that work in every sector of the media." The former POTUS paused to drop his chin so his eyes stared upwards at Karen, such to intonate the gravity of the situation, and not just the weight of what was in his head. "As you know, this year in 2018 my wife and I have setup our own production company, Sir Duke Productions. Furthermore, Art via his ownership of Excursion Group is moving beyond just talent representation and into studio representation and management - content creation. As we are known to the public as affiliated with `the Inner Party`, this is the overt side. The covert side is where you come in."

Karen had always adored _Charlie's Angels_ growing up. Her leaving middle age gave a perfect window to tint a childhood dream as anything but childish - her country may depend on it.

"Karen," the former POTUS continued, "what we're looking for you to do is to help develop a network of like minds in as many control rooms that handle live TV, news, and cable TV advertising as possible, whom either appear apolitical or perhaps even can pose as constituents of `the Other Party`. They'll work their jobs as appropriate, but every now and then, to our discretion and at your leadership, will nudge the conversation in the favor of `the Inner Party`, whispering words for on air talent to repeat, rotating ads in different ways, and more. We're investing in AI technology and research to understand what will best push the country in our direction, without their..."

The former POTUS struggled to find the next word, to which Art interrupted by offering "without their consent?" The Former POTUS sighed and continued in a silent, manufactured acceptance.

"The point is to secretly spread the message of `the Inner Party` in avenues of the media that seem apolitical, such that we can regain control of the country - regain control of the truth. Given how far `the Other Party` has gone in distorting the truth, we feel that something must be done, that we must help heal this country by... administering the truth."

The POTUS paused on those words - _administering the truth_ - with a practiced flourish that spoke of repitition.

Art interupted, leaning in with excitement - "I like to call it AdminiTruth. Get it?" Art smiled widely and and his eyes lit up.

The former POTUS and Art's husband, Roman, both gave Art the same tired look, of a parent whom knew their child had gone askew, which Karen was too rapt in excitement to notice. The former POTUS, as well as Roman, especially Roman, cringed at the joke which they could not get Art to let go of, but they both knew how difficult it was to explain literary references to him without inciting his anger, and decided to let it go.

...

And that was the dawn of AdminiTruth. For the first few years of secret meetings, people tried to get Art to drop the nickname, but he was simply too proud of his portmanteau to do so. Furthermore Art would say, no one ever made a good movie out of _1984_, so who cared? It was funny.

So the group kept the name, keeping Art happy and on an even keel quickly grew to be more important than any other type of logic.

The group had developed a set of cultural defense mechanisms whenever someone drew a likeness to the Orwell novel and what they were doing. The main tactic was simply to point out the obvious - that it wasn't _exactly_ like _1984_, as though being a little evil wasn't being evil in and of itself, it was just, well, "mini evil".

In our Country, there were two parties after all to _1984_'s one, ignoring Orwell's point of there being two other super powers outside of the fictional Oceania. Furthermore, they weren't _torturing_ the Man - at least not yet, if you joked to Adria in the right way. The important part was to always say these points with an air of mirth and condescension, as though what you were saying was, well, kind of _nuts_. They were all working together to misdiagnose The Man, so while this was always said with a laugh and a smile, it was said with teeth - do you really want to try and bite the hand that feeds?

It never came to it, but just in case Karen and Raquel had developed a plan in case anyone drew an allusion to these deflections as a form of Orwell's doublethink. You were supposed to laugh - hard - at how ridiculous that was, and then just say "well, don't think twice about it, it'll be alright" hoping that the shallow intellectualia of the most obvious Dylan reference in the world would gloss things over. And if that didn't work - they'd have to be fired. It was a breakup song, after all.

But needing to deploy the Dylan reference never came to pass. Years of `the Inner Party` having ignored the lessons of _Manufacturing Consent_ made it easy on AdminiTruth to not have to deal with such a thing, though they were prepared to.

Karen smiled, feeling slick in front of the mirror, seeing her multi monitor setup of the man's house in the background, her notes on his parent's behaviors to a side, her long list of advertisements that correlated to The Man's researched emotional triggers, the list of semiotic malaproproisms they'd use to help them misdiagnose The Man, and a preparatory list of AdminiTruth journalists and bloggers ready to drop a headline with a cute reference to mislead The Man, whom preferred reading to watching TV, when needed. One thing Karen never took for granted was that she was never alone when working for AdminiTruth - they had people in every sector of the media, most of which were not overtly political at all - it was the perfect cover.

They were prepared for everything. Getting into The Man's head such to establish control of the situation he had put `the Inner Party` in was a first step towards getting into the country's head for the better of `the Inner Party` - for the better of `the Inner Party`'s truth.
