## 10.8.2023

### Delaying a Declassified Denouement

The Man stared out of the glass sliding doors in the living room of his childhood home, which the situation had returned him to. It had taken some getting used to, but after two months, it wasn't really an adjustment; it was like living in a place he had never been before, emotionally speaking. It was indeed his childhood home - he looked out on a porch made of wood he had stepped on countless times. However, now he was on a network of cameras his parents weren't aware of, though his parents were aware of some type of medical research they were participating in. The Man presumed his parent's participation - which they vehemently denied if he brought it up even as a fiction - was because if his parents could prove with the medical researchers that their son was severely mentally ill, it would allow his sibling's children's custody to be reversed, which The Man presumed as well was due to a divorce caused by the fact that his sibling put him under the medical research, at the behest and control of `the Inner Party`. The Man's sibling and he had a past of competing for their parent's affections, which he had generally won as a child. However, he had lost their trust in his late teenage years, when his sibling was in their early 20s and away from the house. Thus, while The Man's sibling, and not the Man, was the current favorite child - though such terms usually don't indicate one child being able to put another into politically motivated medical research - the sibling never got to experience the light of being "the golden child" as an actual child, so the Man felt their motivations were along that side of emotional distortion.

Or, none or little of that was true and thus he was bat-shit crazy. Then the medical research would prove that, at least eventually.

At any rate - it didn't really feel like returning to his childhood home, so it wasn't really an adjustment - just a new source of tension.

The Man counted his blessings - alongside his very skilled and talented lawyer, he had just a few days past secured relief from the tension of his criminal charges, which were to be dropped in 6 months time as a judge had granted him an adjournment for complete dismissal in a pre trial agreement. This also effectively sealed the charges, provided he kept his nose clean for the next 6 months, which he intended to. So, while the non consensual medical research, along with the network of cameras set up in his childhood home and presumably around the town itself as well - and without mentioning his growing suspicion that PNN anchors did the news for millions of national viewers all while watching him in the camera installed in a air vent above the television his parents watched - were definitely sources of tension, none of them could put him behind bars with the immediacy of a judge's gavel. He held a joint in his hand, and that would help tension wise, but before he struck the flint in his lighter there was something he wanted to consider which he had been putting off.

The discovery process of the court hearing had come and gone, and included with it a declassified FBI document, which was declassified for the purposes of the trial which never happened. He had kept his initial readings isolated to how the ADA, the assistant distract attorney assigned to the prosecution, could use the information against the Man. He didn't want to focus on it more than that until after he had gotten through getting the criminal charges dropped. While the Man was glad to not have to had suffered through an actual trial, he wasn't terribly worried - in a realistic, non politically motivated situation, the NYPD would've likely given him a warning without arresting him, as he had had a clean record. Not to mention he was charged for an act of fiction, amongst other things, and he was pretty sure the courts wanted to avoid setting precedents purely for the purpose of reality television.

Now that he was past the court situation, there were some things in the FBI document that had interested him. He re-read the email attachment on his phone, joint ready to burn in his other hand.

The general counsel for the company he had started working for in 2022 when this whole fiasco began had contacted the Feds in January of this year, 2023. The same company had deep ties with a former administration of `the Inner Party`. Their GC, Kristy Vulfpeck, was quite two faced and shady during his employment and had a lot to say to the FBI, and the Man didn't fully blame her. He had made her and her associates look foolish as he departed the company in March of 2022, emailing her to let the company know they tipped his honeypot social media accounts, amongst other things that would trigger a shame reaction, which he was hoping would lead to a predictable shutdown response - but he didn't know Hollywood was involved.

The Man wasn't aware of _The Mimus_ at the time, though they surely were, and if he was, he would've never acted in the manner to which he did, because then he would've understood their odd behavior and not been threatened by it. _The Mimus_ being involved really ought to have never let this situation get as weird as it did, but `the Inner Party`'s Freudian id being attached to Hollywood let it work to their embarrassment in ways they weren't mature enough to handle. The Man was approaching his 40s, and felt age was irrelevant - he realized others didn't let go of being embarrassed as easily as he does, and he didn't blame Kristy for wanting some kind of vengeance, though he would avoid letting them have it as much as possible, given his current perspective on the situation.

That said, there was someone who spoke alongside Kristy that he didn't know, a Liza Cahoots. He thought he read the name wrong even though it was in all capitals in the FBI document. It was unclear between Vulfpeck and Cahoots who said what specifically via the FBI document, as "just the facts" were written and they were both listed as being interviewed over the same phone call via Cahoots' conference line, so the Man decided to google search Ms. Cahoots to get a better idea of her. When he saw a quote of hers reading "Liza Cahoots? Call me Liza K Hill!" - K Hill being a top firm on K Street in DC - he wasn't shocked. When he saw her face, he was immediately unsettled.

_This woman looks a lot like the woman driving the white Subaru down the block._

Which was complicated - the woman driving the white Subaru had spent time pantomiming her awareness of the Man's vocalizations and happenings in the house, and had clearly been projecting herself to be "on his side." However, if "Subaru" (as he had come to call her in his head) was indeed Liza Cahoots, then at some point, she was trying to get Federal charges pressed against him. The Man thought to his suspicions that the PNN anchors seemed to be making similar pantomimes of being "on his side", something he didn't like to think about it because of how crazy it sounded even as a spare thought in his head, but also because he didn't trust PNN anchors even - or especially - when they did their normal jobs and talked about the news. He made a point to not bring up his suspicions in PNN because even if they were faking as though they were on his side, with all of Hollywood watching the situation as they were, they would at least have to work to his benefit to some degree, which he could use. Thus, if Liza Cahoots was Subaru, there was no benefit in broadcasting a connection there either.

Still, that wasn't the most troubling thing about the FBI document.

Vulfpeck and Cahoots claimed the Man had recorded a video in May of 2022, and uploaded it to a Dropbox folder of his in mid January of 2023. However, there was one thing - he hadn't recorded anything in May of 2022. He did practice a monologue, without recording it, for a play he was considering making, which he recently decided to call _Hamlet: Homicidal?_ He wasn't positive but feared `the Inner Party` had bugged his laptop, and thus his monologue was spoken in a manner that could have gotten the FBI to profile him, such to take over and hopefully shut down the reality show he suspected his sibling of putting him in. Whether it was to their sibling's awareness or not, the Man suspected `the Inner Party` intended to frame him through the show and didn't want to just ride it out. Political frame jobs are actually harder with the FBI's presence, given their responsibility to look into corruption of all kinds. But if the FBI did get involved, or could have in May of 2022, that would only be possible if his laptop for the job he started in that month had been "bugged" - a laptop which was owned by another company entirely than the one Ms. Vulfpeck worked for. It wasn't something he was told of nor was he aware of, so he felt safe in the knowledge that he was practicing a monologue that just happened to be near a laptop a company had given him to work with (though suspiciously had not provided much work for); he wasn't sending any direct messages or recording anything.

But maybe they were recording him - just without telling him, using some kind of hack on his laptop.

Or maybe they were using generative AI to make a fake recording of him - lord knows they've taken footage from enough angles to effectively model his visage and voice. If so, he doubted they used AI for the words and content - `the Inner Party` wouldn't risk their messaging being polluted in such a manipulative act.

But they could have just recorded him without his consent. `The Inner Party` seemed to be into that sort of thing.

Furthermore, while the Man acted verbally via _Hamlet: Homicidal?_ in a way he would never act in reality, it was to get FBI oversight into whatever was happening with the reality TV show he hadn't signed up for. If the Feds did get involved, they couldn't shut the show down, which was a whole other set of questions with politically disappointing answers. But if the Feds wanted him to be crucified as much as (or in tandem with) `the Inner Party`, they clearly didn't play that card. Then again, the FBI isn't known for playing blind man's bluff - that was moreso the Man's thing.

The only thing that was clear was this: the company the Man had started to work for in early 2022 attempted to tell the FBI something they knew to be false, and could only be defended by some kind of Phenomenal hack on Dropbox's servers to falsify an upload at a certain time, which the Man doubted they could have pulled off, but they had at least set themselves up to feel #confident in doing so. The Man was suspicious the medical research intended to prove he had a faulty memory. The only way to say an recording and upload occurred that the Man did not remember doing himself would be to also prove he had a faulty memory.

The Man felt certain Vulfpeck and Cahoots intentionally lied to the FBI, and in a way that seemed to be in tandem with the goals of the phony medical research - whether that could be proven was a whole other thing.

He thought about calling it in. But the Man objected - he wanted this situation to die quietly. If the Feds didn't want to call it out, he didn't. However, if it became important later... maybe he'd tip them off then.

He stepped out onto the porch, closed the glass sliding door behind him, struck the flint of the lighter aflame, and lit the joint he was holding while inhaling lightly.

The Man wondered if he could use the document in his fictional video series, but then demurred. It said "FOUO" at the top - for official use only. It didn't say for fictional use only, but then it didn't _not_ say that either.
