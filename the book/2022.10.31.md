## 10.31.2022

The Man stared up at the ceiling of the hotel room. He had taken it in the hopes of one night's rest without the prying eyes of `the Inner Party`, though doubted he had earned it.

A few weeks ago, he tried making fun of one of their darlings, and one of his personal favorite actors as well, Jorge Floozy (his wife, Abuona, kept her last name). He was hoping Jorge would step in and end whatever this weird situation was, not wanting his name mixed up in some reality show that was beneath his prestige. The Man wasn't sure why Hollywood was even involved, but figure'd `the Inner Party` wouldn't want to risk their reputation or relationship with Jorge.

The Man didn't love it. He was essentially being mean for no reason other than hoping someone of importance to `the Inner Party` would request them to stop harassing the Man in exchange for his, and by extension their, peace.

But it didn't work. They were still a part of his life, whether the Man consented to it or not. Even one of the greatest actors of his time, Jorge Floozy, couldn't convince `the Inner Party` to stop.

_Maybe... I need another figure to falsely clown... maybe someone more influential? Richer, maybe?_

The Man rest assured that when this was over, he would go back to being what he was - a nobody, a yokel. If Jorge Floozy hated him, that really shouldn't mean anything. What importance could The Man have to Hollywood?

The Man felt as though his behaviors would bear no consequence due to his own lack of name recognition, unbeknownst to the roots of the situation that `the Inner Party` and the Wablonsky sisters felt it wiser to hide from him.

_Hopefully said person can get `the Inner Party` to back off where Jorge couldn't. Just who to pick?_

The Man picked up his phone to listen to music, and on shuffle, Jimmy Ruffin came up.

_Hmm... maybe I could go back to a former schtick... There's probably no harm to me in being seen as another nutbag with a strong opinion on a rich famous person. I'm a yokel, a no one, after all._
