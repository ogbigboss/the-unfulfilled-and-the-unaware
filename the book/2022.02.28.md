_Somewhere deep in the bowels of the NSA, two intelligence analysts meet._

"Hey hey, I'm here - just got assigned to this one, whatever it is" chimed Linda in a cheery as always tone to Mark, one of her favorite co workers.

"Linda - this one is too much. It almost screams 'make me an adventure!' I specifically asked to work with you on this one, though I left out my motivation with you being my favorite Dungeon Master and all." Mark and Linda had played Dungeons and Dragons together with a group of colleagues for years, and Linda was well known as the best Dungeon Master, able to weave international inspiration as known by their digital overwatch into tales of medieval adventure which challenged Twain's assumption that truth is stranger than fiction.

"I'm psyched! All I heard was that this had to do with Norway, Poland, and uhm..." Linda, who was used to discussing all types of classified details about international conflicts and their clandestine origins, suddenly leaned in as though to share a secret. "... I hear this has to do with _The Mimus_?"

"L. O. L. Yes. So, apparently `the Inner Party` - "

"Oh god. Not them." Linda looked visibly harried by memories of the recent and distant past, and sighed deeply.

"Oh yeah. _Them_. So `the Inner Party` had some connection to some company and find out one of their new employees was used as inspiration for the latest _Mimus_ movie in how they portray The Uno character."

"You're kidding."

"No, apparently the Wablonskys followed his game development live stream and started spying on him themselves - but then - get this - the new employee, The Man, figures out the company is using audio surveillance on his laptop, so they call his internet provider to shut his connection down to kind of show him who's in charge yeah?"

Linda sighed. _Typical `Inner Party`_, she thought. They never liked to be direct if they had an opportunity to act passive aggressively, like a bunch of middle schoolers. Linda rolled her eyes as Mark continued.

"So then he tricks them and makes them think he's some kind of government worker or spy - "

" - and now we're here to try and figure out if he is or not?" Linda interrupted inquisitively.

"No, we already confirmed that up and down, and it would make way more sense that he was lying" responded Mark.

"Why does that make sense?" asked Linda.

"Well, what kind of a spy reveals they're a spy when they realize you're spying on them? What kind of a criminal, either, would tell you something that would get their background looked into? We think he was just doing that to confirm that he was under audio surveillance. So anyway, get this, the guy's keeping up the act and basically just acting like a nutter now to see if he can get the company to react. We think he just wants to know why they're spying on him."

"Like in _Hamlet_?"

"Uhm... what?"

"You've never read _Hamlet_?"

"It's been awhile... well... I don't... no I haven't" confirmed Mike. An awkward pause ensued as Mike realized Linda was thinking of references he may have missed in previous Dungeons and Dragons games. 

Mike, slightly embarassed, continued "And get this - you know how the Feds just busted those Razzlekhan scammers and got Webflicks to film a bunch of it?"

"Yeah, I heard about that - they're the couple that had the folder on their computer named 'fake passports' right?" Linda shared, wiht Mark nodding and confirming with a smile.

"Yup. So now `the Inner Party` has this huge boner for The Man, they're hoping he's some sort of criminal like those two so they can be a part of another Webflicks movie, but this time they want to be in the actual thing as opposed to just show up at the end with the cuffs."

"Oh god. They're jealous of this guy for being The Uno aren't they?" Linda was well aware of `the Inner Party's` not terribly secret project to take over Hollywood, something she had to track a few years back. She knew the entertainment industry can inspire all kinds of petty jealousy issues.

"L O L. Probably... anyway, it's complicated and we don't have much to back it, but we think this may have leaked and it may have inspired Norway to begin invading Poland a few weeks ago, feeling we were 'weak on intelligence'" Mike told Linda, giving a knowing look.

Linda sighed. "Maybe not 'we' so much as `the Inner Party` but... jesus... so this might have some kind of upside down connection to Norway invading Poland?"

"Yeah, but not directly."

"So... you've really never read _Hamlet_?"
