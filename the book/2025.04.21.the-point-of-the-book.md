### 4.21.2025

## The Point of This Book

> He that troubleth his own house shall inherit the wind: and the fool shall be servant to the wise of heart.
>
> Proverbs 11:29

> "Narcissus does not fall in love with his reflection because it is beautiful but because it is his. If it were his beauty that enthralled him, he would be set free in a few years by its fading."
>
> ― W.H. Auden, _The Dyer's Hand and Other Essays_

> "It is said an Eastern monarch once charged his wise men to invent him a sentence, to be ever in view, and which should be true and appropriate in all times and situations. They presented him the words: 'And this, too, shall pass away.'"
>
> ― Abraham Lincoln

In a world where we're all somewhat narcissistic - our obsessions over our self images as portrayed in our online profiles, the media should we be part of it, and the cameras and devices that increasingly surround us without our consent or knowledge - it only makes sense.

The personality disorder is named after a Greek myth, where Narcissus falls in love with his own reflection in a river, eventually falling in and drowning to his death. There's some other stuff about Zeus and a nymph but they're not as important.

`The Inner Party` alongside the Pequod News Network had fallen prey to the same fate as Narcissus; they were drowning in the river of their own self congratulation, and in doing so, spending three years and losing two elections using all of their political capital earned via their past achievements towards an effort of narcissistic retaliation in sabotaging The Man's life, `the Inner Party` had also sabotaged their own voter base, their own country, themselves - they were drowning in losses, and yet were unaware of why [^1].

They would read this and simply say `the Other Party` was far worse, The Man was far worse, the media was far worse (outside of them), the Scopes trial which _Inherit the Wind_ was based on was actually about universities and education while ignoring how they had repurposed those as weapons in their war against The Man - and on and on and on.

A key component of narcissistic personality disorder is the inability to blame or hold oneself accountable, and it paired nicely with intellectualism and politics [^2].

The Man arrived at this conclusion - that in a world where everyone is forced to be somewhat narcissistic, that what we were all experiencing was a form of evolution, one that required life experience and was accelerated by technology instead of genetics.

It wasn't that `the Inner Party` and the Pequod News Network were fully wrong to point the finger outward - yes, the other "side", whichever they picked at the moment, were certainly narcissistic.

It's just that in a world where we're all prey to narcissistic personality disorder, to avoid the fate of Narcissus, the trick to surviving the technological evolution is to learn to tread water [^2]. To recognize your grandeur could be shared at best, and at the least, not harm others as you bask in your own glory.

To manage your condition [^2].

Or, if fame is a drug, and narcissism a symptom of abuse, then `the Inner Party` still hadn't learned to handle it's shit; but maybe a future version could [^3].

For now, thought The Man, if `the Inner Party`, the Pequod, Raquel, Art, whatever and whoever, were to drown, then so be it. It's Charles Darwin's truth brought to a cultural plane of existence.

Like so much unconquered trauma, the wind would not relent - it would be inherited. If not soon, then eventually.

The Man was depressed at the moment, more than he had ever been in his lifetime. He had been this way for over three years now, three years of `the Inner Party`'s unchecked narcissistic rage hellbent on sabotaging every conceivable part of The Man's outer and inner lives. Still, he found his happiness in playing the fool - it was easier than constantly managing his own suicidal ideations caused by `the Inner Party`, at least. And ultimately, this situation was not his to sow, it was theirs.
