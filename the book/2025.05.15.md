## 05.15.2025

> "Children know perfectly well that unicorns aren't real, but they also know that books about unicorns, if they are good books, are true books."
>
> ― Ursula K. Le Guin, _The Language of the Night: Essays on Fantasy and Science Fiction_

The Man had first thought of the idea, and even put some work towards it, late last summer.

Why not write a book fictionalizing what he was going through?

He came back around to the idea late last March; mostly as a threat. He watched the film _Croupier_ - which ends in the main character writing a book about the movie's happenings - to let the Man's detractors know of his intentions. But it wasn't until he had put together - or perhaps, had stopped resisting himself from putting together such that someone above his rank in society could finally conclude this chapter in a way where he could avoid doing so - the idea that classified information drove his detractor's ambitions.

This started in 2022, and unless things were declassified before that - and it's unlikely _everything_ would be, even if _somethings_ would - then he and the world would have to wait until 25 years later, or 2047, for the full story to start becoming declassified as per automatic declassification standards. He'd be 61.

The Man was suddenly grateful for all the celebrity inferences with birth dates - as a result, he knew some of their ages. Mike Myers, who the Man recently saw on the cover of a movie, was 61 as of today this year, in 2025.

The Man made a note to look into Mr. Myers's fitness and health regimens at some point - if The Man has to wait until he's 61, he'd prefer to remain as good looking as possible. The Man also kept in mind that he realistically only saw Mr. Myers with makeup, lighting, and Photoshop on his side - it wasn't the real Mike Myers, but something else. The Man wondered if maybe in 22 years, there would be some kind of automated real life version of that holy trinity - makeup, lighting, and Photoshop - but figure'd it would be better to keep fitness and diet more in mind.

`The Inner Party` spent three years defaming the Man, and at much cost to themselves, and all to prepare for a future where their sins would find them out in a quarter century's time. The working age of many politicians in the present moment spanned past the 70s into the 80s, with some in their 90s. The Man sighed, thinking of all `the Inner Party`'s luminaries, yet to ripen at the age of 60.

They'd never stop coming for him in between now and whenever the truth gets declassified.

The Man had already written quite a bit, but always with an air of facetiousness - he posted things online to a location which wasn't in a public forum like social media, but just a place where you'd have to know the link, knowing that many in the media and more were reading to get his take on the situation. In many cases, the Man was not motivated in earnest, but in a gambit similar to Hamlet's putting on of the play _Mousetrap_, all be it with certain edits, to help him and Horatio ascertain whether Claudius was the villain of the play _Hamlet_. Similarly, if The Man wrote something of interest, his gaslighters, their employees, and his onlookers couldn't help but betray their reactions in ways the man could perceive - their tensions, their emotions, these were things they couldn't hide from the Man so long as they couldn't hide themselves or the people in their employ. And they always needed to show themselves - how else could they attempt to gaslight him?

In other cases, the Man wrote just to keep a log of the truth. Sometimes he begged for rescue, though that was also something of a _Mousetrap_ move, albeit with a reveal he wouldn't terribly mind.

And, in some cases, he wrote towards the exercise of _writing_ - he was living in a moment of history, he always knew that, and while that in and of itself was interesting, it was what the moment _said_ about the world, the age, the times, that was interesting - isn't that what motivates most writers anyway?

Last August he had started an idea, and titled it _The Unfulfilled and the Unaware_, such to ask the question - why would so many people do this to him? His only answer was that some were unfulfilled, that they only sought to devour and nothing more, with a zombie-like bottomless hunger that was so tied to their survival such to justify sacrificing the rest of their humanity. Others thought it was fun or amusing to try and destroy him, but were unaware to what it said of themselves.

But, the Man had tricked `the Inner Party` - it was a con, it just became bigger than anything he could have seen coming, because at the time he wasn't aware of _The Mimus_ and his relationship to it, as well as their awareness. He didn't like to think of himself as unaware, at least not in the context he was aiming for with the title.

Picking the book idea back up a month ago, The Man retitled it to _The Unfulfilled, the Unaware, and I, Confidence_, in reference to the film _Croupier_, whose main character ends the movie by writing a book called _I, Croupier_. He wasn't sure if any publisher would agree with this choice, but that wasn't the matter.

He needed to write - he had 22 more years before the truth would be declassified, unless it happened sooner, but he couldn't count on that. All he knew was that for the next 22 years, `the Inner Party` would stop at nothing - they already had showed little restraint - at persisting the cover up of their narcissistic revenge, ignoring it was their original sin all along. They would frame him, defame him, smear him, cast further false allegations, such that when the truth came out, they could have a story to tell about why it was false.

His only hope was to keep writing about it, such to offer a counter to that day, whenever it arrives. _The Unfulfilled, the Unaware, and I, Confidence_ would have to be just one of those writings, followed by two decades worth of more. The Man could rejoin the real world then, hopefully.

_I just need to look like Mike Myers. But with darker skin._

The Man was somewhere he had been this whole time - he had not wanted to even suspect any of this, and hoped he wouldn't have to find out, and was caught in between. He looked into his mirror from an angle, and the fading sun from the window against the lack of light in his room only showed half of his face with clarity.

_Dammit, I'm not a computer nerd with a box set of_ The West Wing _on my desk, I'm a computer nerd with a box set of_ Sunburned Spies _on my shelf._ thought The Man, realizing he was boxed into between surrealities yet again.

He was writing all of this to a location viewable on the internet, and known to most of hte media tracking his situation, as well as both `the Inner Party` and their counterpart, `the Other Party.` The goal of that was to try and control the situation, and with any luck, his writing could trigger a change to occur sooner than in over two decades time. The Man had little hope for luck, however.

The Man was cursed. He was put into a movie without being told, the movie portraying society as a false reality, and the Man had to live an actual false reality until he was Mike Myers's age, unless `the Inner Party` killed him before that. But why would he be so lucky? He was cursed.
