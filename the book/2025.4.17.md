## 4.17.2025

There was something The Man couldn't figure out, or at least he couldn't figure out what it meant.

He estimated that for over a year, he was accused of something he would never do, but he wasn't aware of the accusation. Somewhere in March of '24, the Polonium Reality TV crew chose to hint at it, and then he surmised it. And then on May 19, 2024, a date he would never forget, he shared the story of a car theft which proved his innocence. This also, to many, proved the accusation was manufactured to be reversed as an insurance policy, most likely to protect `the Inner Party` from his accuser eventually catching a conscious.

That wasn't what bothered him, well, not entirely.

While he was accused and there was some question as to whether or not the accusation was truthful, it never seemed like anyone felt threatened by The Man, so much so that before and after he realized he had been accused (though, even to this date, he's not 100% sure of it, or anything), people were nice to him. Sure, it was something of a front, and the "nice" side was just half of the Polonium crew and whoever they worked with projecting a false competition through a legal disciplinary situation which should have been confidential, but they _were_ nice. They were pretending to be on his side, something The Man catered to so there could at least be a "fake nice" ending to this horrifying Hollywood story he had been cast into via abduction by means of fraud.

But, after May 19, maybe a week after, everything changed. Those that were nice to him suddenly began to, for lack of a better term, dream torture him. They didn't let him sleep, instead taking advantage of speakers they placed throughout his house, so that anywhere he tried to lay to rest, they could invade his thoughts and force him to think what they wanted him to think, knowing their abduction by fraud left him nowhere to run.

They may have just as well strapped him to a chair, held his eyelids open with tweezers, and forced him to watch films of disturbing images of what they thought of him.

He had considered this may have been a forced betrayal, for the attempt to diagnose him as Borderline, but it took place over a month before the legal discipline hearing started, to the best of his assumptions - it didn't seem correlated or casually connected.

They were dream torturing him for their pleasure, for their mirth. When he called them out for the seemingly casualness of their cruelty, `The Inner Party` got some of their celebrity members, die hard party supporters whose music The Man loved like Jack Legendary, to make albums and songs that treated it like a joke. He thought of the photos of the smiling faces of the Nazis outside Auschwitz, of Abu Ghraib. He knew what he went through wasn't equivalent to the victims, but the smiling faces of those in control in those pictures sung in harmony with the faces he saw outside his window, the ones that were once pretending to be on his side.

That said, this parallel wasn't what bothered him. Though they had mostly abated (mostly), the effects of the dream torture made his every evening end in paranoia before his body forced him to rest, but even that wasn't what bothered him, not at the moment.

`The Inner Party` enjoyed the false accusation - even lying as though they were on his side - as long as it could be debated to be true. Once the truth was revealed, that it was a lie, `the Inner Party` lost what they really were after, which was to destroy The Man's image against an audience he didn't actually care for as much as they did, Hollywood.

Once the truth was revealed, that The Man was innocent, this justified `The Inner Party` to treat him like the punching bag they wanted to make out of him all along.

The Man just couldn't figure out what this said about `the Inner Party`. It put into a twisted light Raquel O'Brien's every accusation of `the Other Party` as "the worse one", The Man recalling the times she and others from the Pequod would - with deniability, of course - remind him that they were in control of the non consensual dream theater.

That said, The Man knew that if they read this, they'd just roll their eyes and make a joke about it like they've been doing with Orwell's _1984_ for over a year now, perhaps for over 40 years now.

He didn't have to think twice about that.
