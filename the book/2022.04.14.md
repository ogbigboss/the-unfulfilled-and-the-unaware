## 4.14.2022

Somewhere in Europe, a yearly gathering of nearly a dozen ex military intelligence types turned lawyers met in a bar. They all had half a year to solve a series of puzzles created by one of them and placed in public locations and media outlets, all meant to give the location and time of next year's meet. Those that got it immediately found cute ways to hint at their colleagues that didn't through their clients and other means, just to make sure everyone who was anyone in their world came to what they were calling their "covert convention." The typically boring world of "business intelligence" liked to find ways to keep themselves entertained between these meetings where they'd share stories they couldn't otherwise tell.

After the person who set up the initial puzzles shared their stories, they drew straws to choose who would have the honor of choosing the location for the next year and dropping hints for them all to figure things out. Their normal affairs were all of private matters for their clients, and they liked it that way. Their business wasn't meant to be anyone's business but their clients. Aside from the convention, of course.

Lately, something threatened that.

"I hear they're trying to make a TV show."

Someone spat out their drink in shock.

"Was that really necessary?"

"Oh... sorry, yeah it was kind of gross. I just get caught up in acting like we're in a movie when we meet up like this."

"Yeah, but, that's the point - they're trying to make a movie out of this. Or a TV show. I'm not sure they've chosen a format yet."

"What are they doing?"

"They're gonna frame some tech worker guy to make it look like he's a hacker, then make themselves the heroes of the show when they 'catch' him."

A collective sigh formed across the group. Their line of business wasn't meant to be public, and framing an innocent civilian wasn't exactly de rigueur.

"... can I guess who 'they' are?"

"Oh, you already know. One of the only firms that hasn't figured out we meet up like this."

"Obsidian, huh?"

"OOPSI themselves."

OOPSI - Obsidian Oval Private Security Intelligencers - was a business intelligence firm with a reputation problem.

"They want to solve their image issue after the Barfey Weinhold debacle."

"Serves them right, working for a creep like that. Could've just left him with his criminal defense lawyers - that's what they're there for!"

They all took a sip of their drinks, ready to change the subject, when the one person amongst them who didn't raise their glass cracked a smile on the left side of their face.

"Well... what if we got in their way?"

"Hmmm... it would actually be in our client's best interest that our industry didn't have a TV show made about the things we do..."

"Not to mention, sparing an innocent person would be a nice change of pace from the usual."

"Then it's settled - let's get a TV show canceled!"

"Who knows, if we start doing good things, maybe we could even create peace in the Middle East!"

Much laughter at such an absurd conclusion proposed to a low brow beginning, and then the plotting began.

"Wait... what would we call ourselves then?"

"What else? Counter-OOPSI!"
