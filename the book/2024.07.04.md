## 07.04.2024

"Fuck, I hate to say it, but the guy got old fast - he's just not mentally capable at this point. It really hurts me to see it." Roman was lying, knowing that President J's loss left an opening for him to eventually run for President, hopefully in 2028 - there's no way he'd enter any kind of race at this point, with only 4 months to go before the vote.

"Same... look... it's most likely VP K will pick up the ticket..." Adria supplied the obvious outcome, and was followed by Raquel.

"... and VP K, or whoever really, has a tough road to win themselves." Raquel sighed as though this was all just hitting her, though, she had realized it months before.

The complicated thing for Roman, Adria, and Raquel was not political, but medical, and legal.

The legal discipline hearing started in NY in a few weeks. They felt confident they could get the board to agree to The Man being schizophrenic - Adria was working the night shift, forcing the Man to experience long term sleep deprivation alongside whatever awful feelings they could force onto him via the dream speaker. With any luck, the sleep deprivation and psychological bullying would combine into a psychotic episode, all while the legal disciplinary board was watching.

However, that wouldn't fully seal the deal against The Man - informed consent could still be a legal issue brought up by The Man. There was only one way at this point to avoid that - a Presidential waiver. A US President can sign a waiver allowing for informed consent to be bypassed in medical research. While the initial executive order that allowed for this was specific to military operations, it's wording left loop holes that `the Inner Party` felt could be bypassed, particularly Roman, who helped write the executive order that allowed for this exception in the 90s. They hadn't talked about this much until recently, as they were relying heavily on the false accusation to be the basis of their false diagnosis of schizophrenia for the Man, and one that was worthy of bypassing informed consent. But that moment passed when the Man proved his innocence via the car theft, giving the Presidential waiver more importance as an opportunity.

Roman spoke on it first.

"Look, we can't ask President J - even if he wins - to make the waiver. It's too risky - his cognitive deficits are likely to become a source of debate, particularly as automatic declassification hits in 23 years. This whole thing will look suspicious if a President who is barely aware of their surroundings signs a medical research waiver in the field of psychiatry of all things."

"And if VP K, or anyone really, picks up `the Inner Party`'s ticket, they're not exactly a shoe in." Raquel repeated, in a different context.

They all paused, staring at each other. After some moments passed, the one with the most bi-partisan experience piped up.

"Maybe... we can get an inside man on `the Other Party`'s side to help us out, should they win" spoke Adria.

"What, like a snitch? We have plenty eyes on the inside of their side" offered Roman.

"No, no, not like a snitch - someone with leverage, that could convince `the Other Party` it would be better to sign a Presidential waiver allowing the Man to be a subject of medical research, with or without informed consent" Adria clarified.

"Jesus - how in the fuck could we pull that one off?" Raquel balked at the suggestion.

"Well... there is someone in this situation that's not exactly broke..." Adria hinted.

Roman and Raquel gawked a bit, then each took a deep breath in tandem as they sat more upright, considering things.

"Huh... we did convince him to spend half a hundred million on that god awful website two years ago" Roman considered.

"Plus, we could've never gotten a hacker into that building without our rich friend knowing if we hadn't of managed convincing him of that acquisition..." offered Adria, thinking of the fun she had with Kristin on the phone with the hacker while they were playing with that database.

"Art tells me that was pivotal in convincing a certain block of these celebrities and journalists that this medical research was warranted" Roman added. "And it's really helping keep them in line while we're doing the dream torture thing."

Raquel lightened up.

"So why couldn't we convince him to support `the Other Party`? A huge donation perhaps? Something to get him on the inside of the administration?"

"And then we have our guy on the inside - someone with leverage."

"We just need to get him to convince them a waiver is more in their interest than exposing us" Roman reminded the group, soberly.

Again, the three sighed a bit, Raquel sipping her beverage, seeking a liquid form of intelligence to guide her. It didn't come easily.

"Well... it's just a backup plan. President J could hold on, and VP K could win."

The three all leaned back, looked away from each other, and picked up their drinks, each of them sipping at first, then quaffing until their glasses were empty of the weight they once contained.

Roman covered his mouth as if to belch, then keeping his mouth covered, used his other hand to tell the ladies he'd be going to the bathroom.

As Roman left, Raquel looked at Adria and pretended to vomit, first covering her mouth, then removing her hand and reducing it to a middle finger, as though it was rejected by her stomach. Adria giggled.
