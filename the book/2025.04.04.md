### 4.4.2025

The clock crept past midnight, the Third of April became the Fourth, and a conclusion had dawned upon the man.

For a little over a month starting in late January, The Man contemplated whether or not `the Other Party` were in fact playing what he had come to call the Apophenia Game. The Apophenia Game involved interspersing words, phrases, and thoughts The Man expressed in privacy, but doing so utilizing the media as well as paid actors who stalked the man in the streets of his hometown without his consent. This was to prove, in all moralities of faith, an argument that the man suffered from bouts of Apophenia, which is to see connections and patterns that did not actually exist, a key symptom of Schizophrenia.

The Man had expressed in several ways that this was a purely bad faith argument - that his only Apophenia was that he saw a pattern of people trying to test him for Apophenia. Therefore if they utilized the Apophenia Game as an argument it would invalidate itself - there was a connection, it was a pattern, it just wasn't the one they wanted to prove. The Man had gotten used to `the Inner Party` doing this alongside what he called their cabal of co-conspirators, AdMiniTruth, utilizing nearly all forms of media to do this, but `the Other Party` had not to this point, at least to The Man's knowledge. Until this past January, that is.

Somewhere in March of 2025 the man made some cursory feints to act as though he believed `the Other Party` were sending him "secret messages" related to DOJ or FBI activity. Almost immediately, `the Inner Party` reacted by paying hired actors whom could have passed for FBI agents to send him two of the usual messages to indicate he could be correct. One man held a red bag with a "target" symbol on it to The Man's right in a grocery store - a way to say "you're right on target." Another man pointed his left hand to his nose while facing the Man and walked into a house on the Man's right in the street - a way to say "you're too right on the nose."

The man had led `the Inner Party` into believing he thought of these as communications, part of his scheme of entrapping them into committing criminal medical malpractice utilizing reality TV, or, as The Man called it, "entraptertainment." So he was sure there weren't any actual secret messages, just an attempt to "prove" apophenia.

But why would `the Other Party` participate?

The Man checked himself - he could of course be wrong. Coincidences do happen. He grew up knowing someone who had been shat on by pigeons not once, but twice, the second time mere feet away from The Man, whom at the time was a boy. It didn't mean anything, but it was hilarious - especially the second time. Sometimes lightning strikes twice, after all.

That said - if `the Other Party` was participating, what could it mean?

Being exposed for committing criminal medical malpractice was a severe political threat to `the Inner Party`, and one that was explicitly to the advantage of `the Other Party.` If `the Other Party` was trying to aid `the Inner Party` in misdiagnosing The Man, there wasn't any clear benefit to `the Other Party`, whom had a dominant control over parliamentary votes - there was little `the Inner Party` could offer them in terms of dealmaking.

And then it hit him like a pick up truck that was actually used for a real purpose, full of equipment in it's carrying space that added an extra weight of reality to it's utility.

If `the Other Party`'s DOJ needed to provide their own argument, or counter argument, to the man having Schizophrenia to any degree, then it could only mean one thing.

The call was coming from inside the house.

The Man had realized something which had helped him ascertain the legal discipline scenario of the previous year - that his detractors weren't trying to mis-diagnose him as an offensive threat - it was a defensive maneuver.

And if mis-diagnosing the man was a defensive maneuver on part of `the Inner Party`, then what could they be defending? If `the Other Party`'s DOJ needed to get involved in this way, why would they need to prove anything?

The Man thought about the nature of classified memos, and how at the discretion of the department it was made under, it could be declassified. That if `the Other Party` were to pursue charging `the Inner Party` for criminal medical malpractice, it could necessitate a declassification of memos and communications given how `the Inner Party` began to pursue the man just a little over three years ago.

The Man made a presumption, which he felt was the only way to explain why `the Inner Party` couldn't just leave him alone, and why `the Other Party` needed to play along in order to prosecute in good faith. And this is what The Man presumed to be true: Back in 2022, someone wrote a classified memo describing `the Inner Party` as behaving Schizophrenically themselves - seeing connections that did not exist, perceiving slights that were not there, and reacting violently using the State to intimidate The Man, and all because they were Narcissistically jealous that The Man had influenced Hollywood, when it was the intent of `the Inner Party` in creating AdMiniTruth for the opposite. Narcissistic rage is a hell of a thing.

The Man had never really wanted to know the truth - he just wanted `the Inner Party` to leave him alone as the Founding Fathers intended; but if `the Inner Party` felt it so necessary to abolish American values, then it had to be for something they felt important. The image of their sanity, after having elected a mentally deteriorating man to President in 2020 - something soon to be exposed by journalists Chubbs Peterson and Nucky Buschemi - might be the one thing to bring out the King George in `the Inner Party`.

...

`The Other Party` had actually shifted gears a few weeks ago to the Borderline Game - trying to prove the Man had Borderline Personality Disorder by pantomiming "rejection" in various ways to see if it would trigger observable emotional dis-regulation - though this was unbeknownst to The Man as he didn't check the news that often. For now, he was focused on the Apophenia Game.

The Man sighed - the thing that really bothered him about the Apophenia Game was how restricting it was. At one point, they attempted to get him to contact an old aquaintance named Sam. Easy enough - he didn't trust Sam anyway, so he didn't have to contact him. But, The Man was in the middle of playing _Doom Eternal_'s second expansion pack, whose main character, "The Slayer", had a name of Dr. Samuel Hayden. Would they use that as an argument towards Apophenia? The Man had meant to finish the game this evening, but held back in pondering `the Other Party`'s potential involvement in the Apophenia Game. This also triggered Johnny Cash's "Sam Hall" to get stuck in The Man's head, to which The Man had to remind himself that whatever he did, to not hum that melody.

This had been the man's life for years now - trying to interpret what he thought they wanted to prove, so he could do the opposite, including trying to interpret when they were employing reverse psychology, so he could prevent them from making bad faith arguments with false positives as a modus operandi. And, of course, at times falsely building up their confidence in thinking they were proving something in order to later set them up as a form of citizen's entrapment.

It was tiring, but a life of being a computer programmer helped - most bugs involved something that was "true" actually being "false", and reversing them as necessary, to varying degrees of subtlety.

The Man needed something else to focus on.

Around 8 years ago, he had begun to try and make computer games with his knowledge of programming. His dream game had, for some time now, been a 3D tower simulator, based on one of his favorite games growing up, _Tower Sim_. He had wanted to do this for decades, before he had started programming, before he started looking into game development.

However, `the Other Party`'s leader was associated to towers. Would he be accused of Apophenia for this act, or perhaps brown nosing? In truth, The Man's fascination with towers as a video game concept predated his awareness of `the Other Party`'s leader, despite the latter being more present in the media than the former. But why wouldn't an 8 year old care more about a game than a real estate mogul?

At some point, The Man had to make his own decisions. He decided to get back to his game, called _Tower Story_, a 3D tower simulator where you make your own towers and AI generated stories play out with the tower's denizens automatically based on your decisions.

He'd also continue writing the book you're all reading - he just prayed it would have a conclusion at some point.
