## Honeypot Hamlet

The Man has a slightly paranoid habit of creating "Honeypots" in the form of digital communications. When he decided he was going to try to marry video games with charity, he thought this would be good, as such an activity could invite spying from politicians and others that prefer to talk the talk as opposed to walk the walk.

### What is a Honeypot

A "Honeypot", literally, is used in areas where animals may reside, stealing resources yet remaining hidden from humans. As these animals can range in danger, from raccoons to coyotes to bears, it's useful to identify them before you may actually come across one. One technique is to leave a literal pot of honey out in the open. This forces the animal to go into the pot, or tip it over, and then use it's paws to get that honey. Then, the honey leaves marks of their paws, and you can use this to identify what type of animal is lurking in your garage etc.

A "Honeypot", digitally, is similar though often more technical[^1]. A "Honeypot", in "espionage" terms, is less technical and often social[^2]. The Man's Honeypots are a bit of a balance.

### The Man's Honeypots

The Man has spent time with various internet accounts that are linked back to him in different ways, and has either posted or sent messages that are meant to be easy profiles of stereotypical behavior, focusing on politics, race, and gender stereotypes, with sprinklings of mental illness.

He tries to write such that once discovered via spying, it would be hard _not_ to test whether they're real, similar to espionage style "honeytraps"[^2], but without sex.

The Man's Honeypots are tripped over in ways made obvious to him at the start of this fiasco - February 2022 - so they served their purpose; he knew he was being digitally spied upon.

### The Man's Honeypots turned against him

The Man made these feeling that those that would try and fail to profile him using the Honeypots in real life would give up quickly after failed attempts. At first inference, that seems to be the case, and the only evidence of them being "tipped over" is in February and March of 2022.

In April of 2022, the Man makes a point to tell his therapist over a web-session of this technique, feeling `the Inner Party` may be listening via the DoD or DoJ they've put onto him. He's pretty confident _someone_ was listening. Although there is some shady suggestive behavior in May of 2022, none of it directly relates to his Honeypot accounts.

Starting in June of 2022, Honeypot tipping seems to come back strongly. Unbeknownst to him, this is because the Dr. Redactada memo has been written and `the Inner Party` decides to use the accounts, which they know are Honeypots, in combination with tactical gaslighting.

This is to attempt to drive the Man insane and "flip" his own tools on him in an act of narcissistic retaliation, as he accidentally made `the Inner Party` feel inadequate in front of the intelligence community, particularly after `the Inner Party`'s behavior against The Man begat the Norwegian invasion of Poland.

### Hamlet-esque

This is kinda like when Hamlet writes a "love letter" to Ophelia to heighten his appearance of madness, which Polonius uses against Hamlet to argue that he is mad, successfully, to Gertrude and Claudius. Simply, this alliterates killerly, excepting situations. Similar to Polonius, `the Inner Party` uses these communications to suggest being love-lorn led to The Man's madness, which works well against claims of BPD, though this argument wears thinner as the years and various forms of abandonment, betrayal, and rejection - real and not - pass, without the Man losing his shit.

### The Honeypots and the False Accusation

As the Honeypots have somewhat humiliated `the Inner Party`, they google around and realize it's history in espionage with "Honeytraps"[^2]. This is brought up in front of Adria, who isn't familiar, and feels inadequate because she's supposed to be Miss Intelligence (ha) as a former CIA officer.

To compensate, she then comes up with the idea of a false accusation such that they can later tell the Man they used his techniques against him, but in a way that shows they knew more about its' history. This works in tandem with `the Inner Party`'s anger over The Man's usage of _Mean Girls_ against them, though he (as of 2022) did not know it was largely inspired by _Jawbreaker_, the latter of which involves a false accusation.

`The Inner Party`'s NPD as exemplified by exploitative behavior when their inadequacy is triggered prevents them from realizing they're going way too far as an acting class of leadership in abandoning the base principles behind their political platform, even considering their motivation to reverse the Redactada memo.

The Man does explain these in a video series he makes in early 2023, but is ignored or seen as rationalizing after the fact, particularly against the false accusation.

He waits after he proves the accusation false as well as after like, nearly 3 full years of "experiments" made to validate these are not Honeypots and actual evidence of mental illness, to bring it up again, feeling `the Inner Party`'s inability to qualify their privacy invasion as a way to diagnose someone over nearly 3 years might push the argument in his favor.
