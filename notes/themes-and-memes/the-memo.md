## The Memo

### How Dr. Redactada gets to "the memo"

Late May 2022, third week: 

In late May, The Man is expressing violent thoughts over corporate audio surveillance while `the Inner Party`, led by Raquel's new production company, is attempting to make a "candid camera" Reality TV Show. Raquel and `the Inner Party` were hoping to frame The Man in June, while telling their Hollywood associates in May the show was about _The Mimus_ and they just "happened" to catch The Man in criminal acts. However, The Man feels paranoid of being framed and talks violently with the aim of getting the FBI involved so there's a larger scope of authority, knowing the Bureau also handles corruption, such to get `the Inner Party` off his back, not knowing they are motivated by _The Mimus_, which The Man is unaware of.

However, The Man is suspicious instead of ignorant to their presence, and as he went through what felt like a Federally assisted investigation at his previous job in February / March of that year, in combination with his own sibling who worked for the Federal Government and was extremely shady despite their estrangement, The Man feels that escalating the situation in a non criminally indictable way is his best shot at protecting himself, especially consider he is suspicious of his sibling's involvement, whom later frames him alongside `the Inner Party` later that year, meaning his suspicious weren't unfounded.

The Man knows he's not committing any crimes and hopes this ends in the FBI confronting him on the issue, which he could use to qualify the situation.

Later, for years, the footage of The Man acting this way is used by `the Inner Party`, Raquel, and The Flying Monkeys to convince others that he's actually insane, when they are well aware he was acting, something he expresses continuously through 2022 to try and get them to leave him alone.

Raquel and `the Inner Party`, not wanting to back down due to their inadequacy issues and jealousy over _The Mimus_, request the FBI's NCAVC do a profile on The Man "just like in those TV shows _Criminal Minds_ or _Thought Patrol_", and take some snippets of the DSM to suggest the Man must be diagnosed.

Dr. Redactada is asked to lead the profiling, and hates those TV shows.

Normally, criminal profiles involve a crime scene, if not several, and victims, and is used to profile a potential criminal in a situation lacking suspects. In this case, there is no crime, no crime scene, and thus no real victims. In lue of the latter, Dr. Redactada puts out an open call to anyone who may have felt "victimized", for the purpose of profiling _something_.

Dozens of `the Inner Party`'s 'appointed officials, Senators, and Congresspeople, all hoping to make a TV show out of The Man inspired by the recent _Razzlekhan_ indictments, make time in the last week of May to meet with Doctor Redactada. They agree to recording it such to further their hopes of being Reality TV "heroes."

Some of the elected officials express thoughts that The Man "must have" been some sort of spy in the past, and thus has turned into a mental case after trying to reconnect to society, citing former CIA spy Jim Lawler's quote from a podcast that being a spy "may [have made me] a sociopath"[^1] amongst other things.

Others claim he must be a hacker or a thief of some sort, otherwise he wouldn't have felt threatened. Others claimed he must have been radicalized by "the internet".

Doctor Redactada challenges this saying that numerous DoD and DoJ agencies have not been able to find any evidence that The Man could have been any kind of spy, thief, hacker, or radical, and the agencies had much hands on experience with all three. Furthermore Dr. Redactada, having seen the worst of violent crime scenes, assures each of them there is a marked difference between language - spoken in private - and actual action, and that either way the Bureau would keep a watch on him just in case. However, though this happened between the months of February and April, but the Senators and Congresspeople still feel The Man may be in the month of May - meaning more than three months have gone by where `the Inner Party` feels the DoD and DoJ agencies must have missed something. This period of time qualifies many for delusional disorder according to the DSM-5, for starters.

These interviews, along with a lack of a crime scene and an inability to interview the one "suspect" allow Dr. Redactada to claim The Man is not diagnosable, but `the Inner Party`'s elected officials are, based on the direct statements expressed in the interviews against the DSM.

### The Memo and the False Accusation

Dr. Redactada, attempting to get `the Inner Party` to leave The Man alone, makes it clear that to qualify them fully for schizophrenia, they need to keep this behavior up for 6 months. As she writes the memo in June, the six month period would end at the start of December. This motivates `the Inner Party` to make the false accusation before that, to avoid qualifying for Dr. Redactada's diagnosis by framing The Man into appearing as the actually diagnosable one.

### A possible denoument to the Memo

`The Inner Party`, much like The Man, disqualifies the idea of them being schizophrenic or delusional by admitting they were lying: they knew the Man wasn't a spy, thief, hacker, or even a threat due to mental illness, and they were just hoping to use their societal status to bend the truth so they can get on Webflicks. This is only possible by admitting to having NPD, driven by inadequacy issues that stem from losing elections to a celebrity, while also having jealousy issues due to a certain unnamed congresswoman's ulta popular weblicks documentary. These inadequacy issues are the reason `the Inner Party` lied to the extent of seemingly qualifying for schizophrenia or delusional disorder.
