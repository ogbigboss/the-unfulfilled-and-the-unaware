## Norway Norway Norway

`The Inner Party` has for years been falsely accusing Norway of spying and "hacking" US elections against their favor despite any actual evidence. This happens before the events of the novel and is explained as exposition in Raquel's opening chapter.

### Norwegian - Polish conflict

Near the beginning of the novel, Norway invades Poland. This is an allusion to _Hamlet_. There is no more desirable "Denmark", though maybe there can be something hinted at down the road. Part of `The Inner Party`'s need to frame the man is to cover their tracks in possibly allowing the conflict to start by their pursuit of The Man (see Roman's notes). That said, it should always be noted that `the Inner Party` is far more driven by The Mimus and a want to frame the man as a "hacker" in reflection of their inadequacy issues stemming from their inability to validate lies, causing more lies as justified by their narcissistic personality disorder, which originates due to their inadequacy issues rooted in their lies - a cycle of avoidance. Though the conflict, which has cost millions of lives, is perhaps the largest responsibility they are avoiding, it is not nearly as driving a force as their want to make a Razzlekhan sequel.
