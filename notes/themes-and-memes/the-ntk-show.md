## The NTK (Need to Know) Show

The NTK show is a private weekly live stream (Fridays at 9PM ET, 6 PM PT) for the entertainment industry to gossip about The Man's predicament

### Origins

The Man has also been recording videos to a private dropbox folder commenting on his life and the situation in a way which counters the narrative of Raquel O'Brien and The Flying Monkeys.

In riposte, they create The NTK Show so they can control the narrative. It offers a well produced alternative to The Man's lo-fi videos, and gives Raquel and The Flying Monkeys a way to control perspectives.

### How The NTK Show controls the narrative

Many stop watching The Man's videos and statements directly, knowing they'll be summarized on the NTK Show.

The NTK show is hosted by 5 people:

1. Gary, someone who is openly defensive of The Man. Unsure of details at this point, but they're definitely one of the novel's gay heroes.
2. A Flying Monkey who holds nothing back in assuming The Man's guilt, though they know it's been manufactured.
3. Dan & Ellie - a couple, Dan about 5'11", white, brown hair slicked back and to the side, and Ellie, about 5'3", white, blonde hair that was straight in August of 2024 and is in curls as of May of 2025. They are meant to be "objective", but are secretly Flying Monkeys, and their role is to always leave The Man open for doubt while appearing like they don't have a horse in the race, so to speak. They do the interstitial stuff, a "talk soup" like review of the Man's week (complete with privacy invading clips), as well as opening and closing monologues.
4. Raquel O'Brien herself - she has the time as she only works one day a week at PNN, but more importantly, this allows her to build an image of "journalistic" "objectivity" wrt to The Man, to hide the fact that she is most responsible for the predicament he's in.

Raquel, the show's secret lead producer, uses her experience at PNN as an expert to portray a popular opinion - in this case, The Man's innocence - but then cast doubt on it, and finally have an "objective" opinion shade towards the doubt, to get an audience to betray something they would otherwise support.

Only one of the show's hosts are truly in favor of the man, another is openly against, but the three in the middle - Dan, Ellie, And Raquel - are all secretly plotting against the Man while trying to appear "objective" and at times in his defense.

### The NTK Show and The False Accusation

Much of the purpose of the NTK show was to create and continue a "debate" about the accusation which the show's creators and most of the hosts know to be false. By simply debating it - even in the Man's defense - it spreads the allowance of accepting that it _may_ be true, even when it is not. Raquel and the Flying Monkeys know this and know that even by acting as though they are "on the Man's side", it actually works to promote what they know is a lie simply by speaking on it. This backfires of course when the man proves it was a lie, and in such a way that points to it being constructed to be reversed by an insurance policy (the car theft).

The NTK's show's purpose in creating a false "debate" is underlined to the reader as it only takes The Man 12 days (May 7 - May 19 2024) to prove it false after connecting that a graduation party may have been said to be involved. Had they told him directly, there would've never been an NTK show.

After the accusation is proven false by The Man, The NTK show draws a sharper focus on gossipping about the Man's sexuality and sexual past, masturbation habits, and other private details of his life, all in an attempt to distract an audience the inner party has spent decades training to accept hyper sexualization as an excuse for allowing defamation of character via privacy invasion to continue, and ignore the mental health hazard this show represents (and in secret, is done on purpose to allow a continuation of gaslighting the man with celebrity complicity).

As the novel enters the fourth act, the celebrity audience growingly understands the NTK show's largest purpose is to distract them from understanding their own complicity in allowing the man to be gaslit in a way that would defend `the Inner Party` from the correct diagnoses in the Memo.

### The NTK Show's faux position against Art

While secretly funding the NTK show, Art is made fun of on it constantly, as is a Hollywood tradition. Not only does this work as a motif - Hollywood types have spent decades secretly and not so secretly bullying Art due to his adult ADHD - it makes Art the "villain" when useful to the NTK show hosts to keep their audience believing the show is for The Man's benefit and not a way to take The Man's voice away while gaslighting him secretly.

This is also used to continue to make some celebrities feel "guilty" for causing the false accusation via their bullying (alongside the Man) of both Art and `the Inner Party` in 2022, when in truth, the situation's roots stem from Dr. Redactada's Memo. Act 4 sees `the Inner Party` and the Flying Monkeys spending their time trying to hide this manipulation.
