## Doctor Fortina Brasilia Redactada

Redactada means redacted in Spanish. Dr. Redactada, one of the novel's gay heroins, is a partial parallel to Fortinbras in Shakespeare's Hamlet.

Dr. Redactada is the FBI agent working in the NCAVC, also a Doctorate in Psychiatry and Psychology, who writes the memo diagnosing `the Inner Party` in June 2022. She's set to retire a year before writing the memo, which she does at the request of `the Inner Party`, who were hoping to use her to diagnose the Man, which pisses her off professionally speaking, and she instead diagnoses `the Inner Party` with narcissistic personality disorder and associated comorbidities, particularly paranoid schizophrenia. Since there is no real "crime scene", she is forced to instead investigate the "victims", whom asked her to use the DSM against the Man, which she couldn't qualify given the events against The Man nearly as much as the DSM worked against `the Inner Party`.
