## NSA Intel Analysts

These characters kind of serve as a "voice of the gods", a mostly impartial though somehow all knowing group of people.

They're very nerdy and play Dungeons and Dragons, eventually leading to them using the novel's events as fodder for their own DnD adventures. It should be hinted that at least one is connected to authoring the NSA's "Untangling the Web" [^1].

They'll often lead the conversation with regards to literary allusions.
