### Roman Auerbach

In 2022, Roman starts and intends to use the pursuit against The Man to sideline the then `Inner Party` POTUS and VPOTUS, viewing both as a threat to his opportunity to run for POTUS. He works to ensure their pursuit of The Man will backfire in ways taht the `Inner Party` will seek to absolve by ending the then POTUS JB and VPOTUS KH's reign. He wasn't prepared for it to backfire in a way that would come to him, and even in 2025, felt confident he could outmanuever such a thing.

Roman did truly love Art in the beginning of their relationship, but starting in the 90s Roman gets Art his first job in talent representation, and since then Roman has hung onto Art as a way to accelerate his own Machiavellian schemes to become POTUS. Art is mostly Roman's tool to develop inroads into Hollywood that Roman can control to better his positioning within `the Inner Party`.

#### Roman's relationship to Pick Up Artists and Camel Cigarettes

In the late 90s, Roman's boss, then POTUS, suffers a scandal due to his sex addiction. Roman, part of the then POTUS' inner circle, comes up with the idea to make sex addiction look cool, and is largely inspired by the 1994 CA Supreme Court Case against Camel Cigs for their Joe Camel character, who caused a surge of smoking amongst minors.

#### Roman's relationship to the Norwegian-Polish conflict

Roman accidentally triggers the Norwegian-Polish conflict by sharing communiques with business associates in Miami, where he was a former two time mayor, and is the location of where one of the Man's bosses in early 2022 works. However, Roman is at the time in Korea as the Diplomat, and because his communiques go through international channels, Norwegian spies learn that Roman is chasing after The Man for being a spy for Norway, when the actual Norwegian spies know this to not be the case. They use this as motivation to begin the invasion of Poland, knowing they can affectively blackmail Roman and `the Inner Party` from getting in the way due to their foney baloney pursuit of The Man being more embarassing, particularly contextualized with their motivations of Razzlekhan + The Mimus.
