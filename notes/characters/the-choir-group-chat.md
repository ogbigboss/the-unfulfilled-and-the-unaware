## The Choir group chat

A group chat of entertainment industry types and celebrities tracked by the NSA

### The Celebrities Timeline

In Act I, they are largely making fun of Art and `the Inner Party` alongside The Man, particularly the latter. The celebs mistake the politicians as being able to "take it".

In Act II, the celebs are taken over by The NTK Show, which is constructed to generate a false debate around The Lie, such to make it seem possible, all while excusing not telling The Man directly about the Lie, such to extend the time the Lie can circulate.

In Act III, the celebs find out the Man is innocent as (most of) they expected, but feel personally guilty that they drove `the Inner Party` into mental instability such to get them to generate the false accusation. Their guilt is used by The NTK show + `the Inner Party` to get them to keep The Man in the grips of `the Inner Party`.

In Act IV, the celebs find out about the memo being real, and slowly accept they've been had all along - they never deserved to feel guilty of being too harsh in Act I, and slowly start to feel sympathy for Art. Those that thought the Man was guilty realize a cover up isn't as strong of a defense as admitting `the Inner Party` lied to them on purpose.
